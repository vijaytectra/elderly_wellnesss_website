What are Alzheimer’s Disease and Dementia?

Before talking about the various care services, it is essential to know more about Alzheimer’s and dementia. Dementia refers to a cognitive condition that includes various problems which affect memory, language, reasoning, problem-solving abilities, and other brain functions. Alzheimer’s disease is the most commonly occurring type of dementia that causes progressive death of brain cells. Both these conditions make it difficult for an individual to connect with people and their surroundings.

Most common symptoms of Alzheimer’s and dementia are as follows:

  1. Difficulty in problem solving
  2. Memory loss disrupting daily life
  3. Confusion with time or place
  4. Changes in personality and mood
  5. Less engagement in social activities.

All these symptoms worsen with increasing age; thus, it becomes essential to provide 24/7 support, which can be physically and emotionally exhausting for families. Hence, using specialized services from Elderly Wellness comes to the rescue and makes living better for your elders.

Necessary Measures Towards Alzheimer’s and Dementia Care

While planning for dementia and Alzheimer related care, it is essential to follow certain measures to make living easier for elders and their family members. The following are some of the most essential measures that one must consider while providing the right care to the seniors:

  1. Personalized Memory Care Programs:

Every individual deals differently with dementia; hence, providing personalized memory care programs is a must. Each person shows different symptoms, lifestyle changes, and emotional needs. Hence, it becomes necessary to provide personalized care plans after a detailed analysis of the senior’s condition and lifestyle. It makes the senior’s everyday living much simpler, and improves their overall health.

  1. Supportive and Safe Environment:

Safety is the number one priority when it comes to handling seniors with dementia. These individuals are at a risk of getting lost, falling, or forgetting important tasks like closing doors or turning off the stove. Hence, proving risk free environment is a must in such cases, and it can be done by using mobility aids, caregiver supervision, and home safety evaluations to identify and address potential hazards.

  1. Well-trained Dementia Caregivers:

The most essential thing to do is to assign well-trained caregivers who are trained in memory care and dementia behavior management. They must be equipped to handle issues like restlessness, paranoia, and aggression. Also, they must provide compassionate services, psychological exercises, and empathetic conversations to create a safer and comfortable environment for the senior citizens.

  1. Engagement and Cognitive Stimulation:

To slow the progress of cognitive decline, mental stimulation plays a major role. Incorporating brain-friendly activities like puzzles, music, storytelling, and light exercises helps seniors to keep the mind engaged and at work. These activities are suggested as per the senior’s interests and comfort level, making their experience enriching and therapeutic, and enabling families to involve in it too.

  1. Essential Support for Loved Ones and Families:

Another essential measure is to provide the required support for families. It must include emotional counselling for families, providing educational resources related to dementia, respite care services, and transparent discussions with family members. All these measures make it easier for families to live in peace and not worry about their loved ones, thus improving the quality of life.

  1. 24/7 Emergency Support for Seniors:

24/7 emergency support becomes very important with the seniors’ increasing age. As a result, the service provider must provide support throughout the day or night. It must help with issues like midnight wandering, medical emergencies, or any other problems. Also, trained caregivers must be allotted to handle such situations carefully and peacefully, reducing the panic caused in the families.

  1. Dementia Support Driven by Technology:

With the integration of technology, it becomes easier to monitor the elderly person in conditions like Alzheimer’s and dementia. Technology offers advantages like health and behavior monitoring, medication reminders, emergency assistance features, and GPS-enabled tracking when seniors roam around. All these features make it easier for families to look after their seniors with ease.

All these necessary measures make it effortless for seniors and their family members to deal with Alzheimer’s and dementia. Additionally, one must take consultation from doctors and experts, and make efforts to make living better for your loved ones.

Services Offered in Adambakkam

1. Home Physiotherapy

Experienced physiotherapists provide therapy for joint pain, mobility issues, post-surgical recovery, and chronic conditions like arthritis or stroke—all at the patient’s home.

2. In-Home Nursing

Qualified nurses assist with wound care, IV injections, medication, post-operative care, and chronic illness monitoring. This helps reduce hospital visits and supports comfortable recovery at home.

3. Assisted Living and Daily Care

For seniors who need help with daily activities such as bathing, dressing, eating, or walking, Elderly Wellness offers personalized assisted living support through trained caregivers.

4. Geriatric and Memory Care

Caregivers are trained to support elderly individuals with dementia, Alzheimer’s, and other age-related cognitive conditions. Services include emotional engagement, safety monitoring, and structured routines.

5. Remote Family Monitoring

Using the Elderly Wellness app, families receive daily updates, vitals, care schedules, and direct chat access with caregivers, making remote care management simple and transparent.

Our Elderly Care Services in Ambattur

At Elderly Wellness, we offer a wide range of services designed to meet the needs of elderly individuals in Ambattur. Our services are aimed at improving both the physical and emotional well-being of seniors, allowing them to continue living independently and with dignity.

1. Nursing Care Services

For seniors who require medical supervision or have chronic health conditions, our nursing care services provide expert, in-home medical care. Whether it’s administering medication, managing chronic illnesses, or wound care, our registered nurses are trained to handle a variety of medical needs. We also offer post-operative care for individuals recovering from surgery.

2. Physiotherapy Services

Mobility issues are common among elderly individuals, whether due to arthritis, muscle weakness, or general aging. Our physiotherapy services focus on improving strength, flexibility, and mobility. Our physiotherapists create customized exercise routines to help seniors regain their independence, reduce pain, and prevent falls.

3. Assisted Living Support

As seniors age, they may need assistance with daily activities such as bathing, dressing, and meal preparation. Our assisted living support ensures that your loved one receives the help they need with activities of daily living (ADLs), allowing them to live comfortably and safely at home. Caregivers also provide light housekeeping and companionship to make the home environment more comfortable.

4. Geriatric Care Services

Seniors with chronic conditions like diabetes, hypertension, or dementia require specialized care. Our geriatric care services focus on managing these conditions effectively, ensuring that seniors remain in good health. Our caregivers monitor vital signs, assist with medication management, and provide emotional support for seniors dealing with age-related challenges.

Most Common Problems in Homes for Senior Citizens

You must recognise the potential areas in your home where seniors are most vulnerable to injury. Some of the most common problems for seniors in a home are as follows:

  1. Slippery bathroom floors
  2. Lack of handrails and grab handles
  3. Tight spaces and inaccessible entryways
  4. Poor lighting and dark corners
  5. Uneven surfaces and throw rugs
  6. Inconvenient shelves and cabinets
  7. Hard to use door knobs

Identifying these problems is the first step before implementing the much-needed home modifications. Considering all these hazards, one must think about the potential home modifications that must be made to make living convenient for the seniors. 

Significant Areas to Consider for Home Modifications

There are several areas, like the bathroom, bedroom, living room, kitchen, and the staircase, where you must make changes to make the living safer for seniors. The following are some of the most common ways in which you can enhance the safety at your home and reduce the chances of injuries:

  1. Bedroom Modifications for Elders’ Comfort:

The bedroom for seniors should be a cosy and comfortable place, where they can rest without any worry. Home modifications in their bedroom must be focused on mobility and convenience. It must have a bed with a proper height to easily get in and out. There must be night lights, bed rails, and a side table to keep essential items within their arm’s reach. All these modifications create a clutter-free and well-lit environment in their bedroom, which encourages peaceful sleep and independent living.

  1. Enhancements in the Bathroom for Safety:

Bathrooms are the most accident-prone areas in a home, as slippery floors, poor support, and tight space increase the risk of falling. Hence, essential modifications must be made in the bathroom. It includes installing grab bars inside the shower and near the toilets. There must be non-slip mats and anti-skid flooring, along with a raised toilet seat can be very helpful. Additionally, installing a shower chair will make it better for the seniors to bathe independently with the help of the support.

  1. Safety in the Kitchen for Seniors:

Kitchens have potential hazards like sharp objects, high storage areas, and slippery surfaces, which increase the risk of injuries. As a result, necessary modifications must be made in the kitchen area too. It mainly includes installing pull-out drawers in cabinets and lowering countertops for wheelchair accessibility. Also, one must place frequently used items at the waist level, and knobs must be replaced with easy grip handles. It makes the kitchen space much safer and practical to use for seniors.

  1. Adjustments in Living Room and Hallway:

As the living room and hallways are mostly occupied with people, they must be open, accessible, and free of any obstacles. You can achieve this by removing throw rugs, installing grab bars along hallways, and replacing glass furniture with safer materials to reduce the risk of injuries. Also, you should opt for firm and supported chairs with armrests for elders’ comfort, and enhance the lighting with LEDS. All these modifications will make your living room and hallway an injury-free space for your loved ones.

  1. Staircase and Entryway Safety:

Having a staircase in your home poses a significant risk of falling for seniors. To reduce this risk, one must make the necessary modifications. One can add sturdy handrails on both sides of the stairs, install non-slip stair treads or carpets, and install stair lifts for seniors with mobility issues. Also, you must improve visibility in the staircase and entryway by using bright lights. All these essential modifications will significantly reduce the risk of falling and injuries in the staircase area of your home.

  1. Monitoring Solutions and New-Age Technology:

With new and advanced technology, it becomes easier for you to improve safety in your home. You can install motion sensors to detect unusual activities, a medical alert system with emergency buttons, video cameras for enhanced safety, and smart thermostats for comfortable living. Along with these modifications, using voice-activated assistants like Google Home or Alexa helps a lot, too. So, use proper technology to make your home much safer for your loved ones, and even children.

All the above-mentioned necessary modifications makes living independent and safer for the elders. Along with these changes, you must also assign a caregiver who will take care of your senior and reduce the chances of falling or injury massively. All these modifications will ensure your peace of mind, along with the seniors’ high quality of living.

Some Cost-Effective Ideas for Home Modification

There are some cost-effective modifications available which you can consider if you are unable to spend a lot of money on expensive modifications. It serves the purpose and ensures safety for your loved ones. Some of the common cost-effective modifications are as follows:

  2. Furniture risers to adjust chair and bed height
  3. Lever handles in place of standard door knobs

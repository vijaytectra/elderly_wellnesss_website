Why is Proper Nutrition Most Essential in Old Age?

With increasing age, seniors face challenges with their metabolism, bone density, muscle mass, and overall body functioning. A poor diet plan can lead to issues like malnutrition, chronic diseases like diabetes, cardiovascular issues, and cognitive decline. As a result, a well-planned and nutritious diet helps them to enhance their immunity, maintain independence, and promote longevity.

There are some major dietary goals for senior citizens. They must maintain a healthy weight, enhance cognitive functioning, boost immunity, eat food that supports their bone and joint health, and aid digestion and proper bowel movement. All these changes makes living healthier for seniors, keeps them active, and reduces the risk of several physical and mental disorders.

Essential Nutrients to Consider for Elderly Diets

Nutrition is the most essential part of any diet. And, when it comes to senior diets, the food must contain all the essential nutrients for their proper health. Hence, focus must be on nutrient-dense food over empty calories. The following are the major nutrients that must be included in every senior’s diet:

  1. Protein:

Protein is the best nutrient for preserving muscle mass and strength for older adults. Seniors must eat food items with lean protein such as eggs, lentils, chicken, cottage cheese, tofu, and fish. It will keep them active throughout the day and help them maintain their muscle mass as they increase in age.

  1. Fibre:

Fibres must be eaten daily as food items high in fibre actively help reduce the blood cholesterol levels and make the digestive system stronger. It can be obtained from fruits (apples, pears, oranges, etc.), vegetables ( carrots, spinach, tomatoes, etc.), whole grains (rice, oats, wheat), beans, and legumes.

  1. Vitamin B12:

Vitamin B12 support the nervous system and its functioning, also it stimulates the production of red blood cells. It can be obtained from fortified foodstuffs, animal products like meat, eggs, and milk. Also, it can be consumed through supplements as its absorption decreases with increasing age.

  1. Potassium:

Potassium helps to maintain blood pressure, bone health, kidney health, and muscle functioning. It is available from food items like bananas, potatoes, beans, and yoghurt. It is an essential nutrient as it supports nerve health, and elders must consume around 3,400 mg of potassium regularly.

  1. Calcium and Vitamin D:

Calcium and Vitamin D are considered the best for improving bone health. They help in preventing issues like arthritis and osteoporosis. It can be obtained from food items like low-fat dairy products, almonds, orange juice, and leafy vegetables. Vitamin D can be obtained directly from the morning sun.

  1. Healthy Fats:

Healthy fats promote healthy brain functioning and reduce inflammation in the body. Some good sources of healthy fats are olive oil, avocados, nuts, seeds, dark chocolate, and fatty fish like salmon and mackerel. Also, it helps in lowering cholesterol and regulating blood supply in the body.

All these nutrients will provide a balanced diet to the seniors and keep them active throughout the day. Also, it will boost their immune system, keep their digestive system healthy, and allow them to function actively throughout the day. Necessary Balanced Meals for Senior Citizens

Now we know about the essential nutrients one must consider for an elderly person. However, it is necessary to plan a balanced meal plan so that seniors get a variety of nutrients through different meals. Each meal must consist of the essential macronutrients and micronutrients. The following are some of the best food items to consume for every meal:

  1. Breakfast:

It can be a heavy meal as it will keep you energised for the day. And, it can consist of food items like oatmeal topped with bananas, seeds, and walnuts, boiled eggs, and herbal tea or a glass of low-fat milk.

  1. Lunch:

It must include all the essential nutrients, and you can choose to eat grilled fish or cottage cheese with rice or rotis, steamed vegetables, salad made with raw veggies, curd or buttermilk, and a bowl of lentils.

  1. Evening Snack:

Evening snacks must be light and good in their nutrient content. You can include fruits like apples, bananas, or any seasonal fruits, herbal or green tea, and whole wheat crackers with freshly prepared hummus.

  1. Dinner:

The last meal of the day, i.e. the dinner, must be light and fill your stomach properly. It must include vegetable soup or khichdi, light salad with olive oil dressing, and chapati with sautéed green veggies.

  1. Bedtime:

This can be an optional meal; however, it will help you sleep well at night. The bedtime meal can consist of warm turmeric milk or a glass of water with soaked chia seeds to improve metabolism.

Following this meal plan will provide the senior citizen with all the essential nutrients and keep their body functions active as increasing age. Also, you can make changes in this plan as per your region and different choices of food, as per the availability and seasonal parameters.

Why is Hydration a Crucial Part of Elderly Diets?

Senior citizens face a major risk of dehydration as they suffer from a declining sense of thirst. As a result, their water intake must be monitored carefully as adequate water intake helps them to aid digestion, regulate body temperature and prevent UTIs and constipation. One must encourage seniors to drink water regularly, offer them fruit juices, soups, and coconut water, and monitor their fluid intake during summers or illnesses. All these factors will enable you to keep your loved ones hydrated.

Particular Dietary Considerations for Seniors

Not every elderly individual can be provided the same diet. In some cases, due to health issues like diabetes, high cholesterol, blood pressure, hypertension, and many more, one needs to make some necessary changes in the diet. The following are some common dietary changes as per the health issues:

  1. Diabetic Elderly Citizens:

Diabetic senior citizens must opt for food items with a low glycemic index, high fibre, and portion-controlled meals. Also, snacks and beverages with high sugar content must be avoided strictly.

  1. Elders with Digestive Issues:

Seniors facing digestive problems must include probiotics like yoghurt, fruits and vegetables, soft-textured meals, and fermented food items (idlis, dosas, etc.) in their diet to promote easy digestion.

  1. Elderly Citizens with Hypertension:

The sodium intake must be controlled in seniors suffering from hypertension. Also, their food must be rich in potassium, and focus must be on DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension) principles.

  1. Seniors with Swallowing Problems:

Seniors with swallowing problems must be served food items in a blended or pureed form. They must avoid dry and hard-to-chew meals and consume fruit and vegetable juices for complete nutrition.

One must consult an expert before planning a balanced diet for senior citizens. There can be several health issues due to which seniors cannot consume all types of food items. Food Safety and Preparation Tips for Senior Well-being

Apart from balanced meals, it is essential to make sure that the food is safe for seniors. Foodborne illness is very dangerous for seniors; hence, proper safety measures must be ensured during food preparation. One must follow the following tips for senior well-being:

  1. There must be proper food hygiene and storage.
  2. Expired food and uncooked food must be strictly avoided.
  3. Meat, fish, and eggs must be thoroughly cooked.
  4. Food must be reheated properly to avoid bacterial growth.
  5. Vegetables and fruits must be properly washed before eating.

All these tips will help you to always serve nutritious and safely prepared food to your loved ones. Also, you can consider using meal delivery or assisted meal preparation services for seniors who are unable to shop for groceries or prepare their meals independently.
